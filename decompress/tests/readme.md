These tests were generated by Claude and used during development to check
the correctness and speed of the Brotli and zlib decoders.

The benchmark data in bench/data/ was generated once on my system (CachyOS)
with bench/make-data.sh. To regenerate it you may need to adjust the paths
in that script for your system.

The test files in vectors/ are copied from brotli-pure (inspect/vectors/): 11
inputs (.raw), each compressed with the reference Brotli encoder at quality 5
and 11 and window sizes 10 and 22 (.br).
